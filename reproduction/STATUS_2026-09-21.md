# iA* reproduction status — 2026-09-21

## Current verified result

The project remains a **partial reproduction**, not a full reproduction of the
paper. The latest result that can be treated as an experiment is the Week 1
32x32 pilot:

- iA* training: 5 epochs, 100 training maps, one training run (the script's
  default training seed was `1234`);
- evaluation: 100 official 32x32 test maps with fixed test-start seed `2026`;
- iA* versus Vanilla A*: 21.4% fewer expanded nodes and 0.81% longer paths;
- CPU runtime: 20.334 ms for iA* versus 13.975 ms for Vanilla A*, a 45.5%
  increase;
- all three evaluated planners achieved 100% success on these cases.

The distinction between the training seed and evaluation seed matters: `1234`
controls the one Week 1 training run, while `2026` controls the sampled test
starts. Therefore Week 1 still has only one independent iA* training run.

## Engineering progress that is not a formal result

Commit `814d0ab` added the controlled multi-seed runner, fixed-case evaluation,
warm-up and repeated timing, rotating planner order, bootstrap summaries, and
tests. A three-seed, 2-epoch, 10-case smoke run is stored in
`reproduction/results/week2_smoke/`. It validates the pipeline only and must not
be reported as the planned stability experiment.

## Known blocker

The official released iA* checkpoint still cannot be loaded because the
downloaded archive is corrupted. The Week 1 report records the matching open
upstream issue. Until a valid official checkpoint or checksum is published,
the reproduction must rely on locally trained checkpoints and disclose this
limitation.
