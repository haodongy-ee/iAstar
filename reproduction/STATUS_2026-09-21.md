# iA* reproduction status — completed 2026-09-21

The planned stability experiment was completed early at the user's request.
This repository is now considered **finished and archived as a partial
reproduction**. No additional iA* experiments are planned.

## Final completed scope

- Week 1 pilot: one 5-epoch run on 100 training maps.
- Final stability study: three independent training seeds (`1234`, `2026`,
  `3407`), 20 epochs per seed, all 800 official 32x32 training maps.
- Evaluation: the same 100 official test maps and sampled starts
  (`eval_seed=2026`) for every checkpoint.
- Timing: three warm-up calls, five measurements per planner and case, CUDA
  synchronization when applicable, and rotating planner execution order.
- Outputs: 900 per-case method records, nine per-run method summaries,
  aggregate mean ± standard deviation, run-level bootstrap intervals, paired
  comparisons with A*, and a final comparison image.

## Final headline result

Across the three training seeds, iA* achieved 100% success, reduced expanded
nodes by 21.43% relative to Vanilla A*, and increased path length by 0.90%.
It did not improve runtime on this CPU implementation: median latency increased
by 124.93% relative to Vanilla A*.

See [`FINAL_REPORT.md`](FINAL_REPORT.md) for the protocol, complete results,
limitations, and interpretation.

## Unresolved external limitation

The official released iA* checkpoint remains unloadable. Upstream Issue #2 is
still open and reports the same missing zip central-directory error. The final
study therefore uses locally trained checkpoints and does not claim to verify
the paper's released model or reproduce all paper-scale experiments.
